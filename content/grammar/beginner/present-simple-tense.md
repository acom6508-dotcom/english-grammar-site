## What is the Present Simple Tense?

The present simple tense is one of the most fundamental tenses in English grammar. It's used to describe habits, routines, facts, and general truths. Mastering this tense is essential for effective communication in English.

## When to Use Present Simple

### 1. Habits and Routines

We use the present simple to talk about things we do regularly:

- I **wake up** at 7 AM every day.
- She **drinks** coffee in the morning.
- They **go** to the gym three times a week.

### 2. Facts and General Truths

The present simple describes things that are always true:

- Water **boils** at 100 degrees Celsius.
- The Earth **revolves** around the Sun.
- London **is** the capital of England.

### 3. Permanent Situations

Use it for situations that don't change:

- I **live** in New York.
- He **works** as a teacher.
- They **own** a restaurant.

## How to Form Present Simple

### Positive Sentences

**Formula:** Subject + Base Verb (+ s/es for he/she/it)

Examples:
- I play tennis.
- You play tennis.
- He plays tennis.
- She plays tennis.
- It plays an important role.
- We play tennis.
- They play tennis.

### Third Person Singular Rules

For he, she, and it, add **s** or **es**:

1. Most verbs → add **s**: work → works, play → plays
2. Verbs ending in -s, -x, -ch, -sh, -o → add **es**: wash → washes, go → goes
3. Verbs ending in consonant + y → change y to **ies**: study → studies, fly → flies

### Negative Sentences

**Formula:** Subject + do/does + not + base verb

Examples:
- I **do not** (don't) like coffee.
- She **does not** (doesn't) speak French.
- They **do not** (don't) work on weekends.

### Questions

**Formula:** Do/Does + subject + base verb?

Examples:
- **Do** you like pizza?
- **Does** he play guitar?
- **Do** they live nearby?

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Forgetting the 's' for Third Person

❌ Incorrect: He work every day.
✓ Correct: He work**s** every day.

### Mistake 2: Using 's' with Negative and Questions

❌ Incorrect: Does he works here?
✓ Correct: Does he work here?

❌ Incorrect: She doesn't likes coffee.
✓ Correct: She doesn't like coffee.

### Mistake 3: Using Present Simple for Actions Happening Now

❌ Incorrect: Look! It rains. (happening now)
✓ Correct: Look! It **is raining**.

✓ Correct for general fact: It rains a lot in Seattle.

## Time Expressions with Present Simple

Common time expressions used with present simple:

- **always**: I always brush my teeth.
- **usually**: She usually arrives on time.
- **often**: We often visit our grandparents.
- **sometimes**: He sometimes forgets his keys.
- **never**: They never eat meat.
- **every day/week/month**: I exercise every day.
- **once/twice a week**: I play tennis twice a week.

## Summary

The present simple tense is essential for describing:
- Regular habits and routines
- Facts and general truths  
- Permanent situations

Remember to add 's' or 'es' for third person singular (he/she/it), and use 'do/does' for negatives and questions.
